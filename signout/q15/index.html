Key points
  • Pheochromocytoma comes from chromaffin cells of the adrenal medulla, which derive from the neural crest, like melanocytes.
  • The adrenal cortex is mesoderm. Thyroid follicular cells and hepatocytes are endoderm. Anterior pituitary is surface ectoderm (Rathke’s pouch).
  • Brother with a thyroid tumor at 30: think medullary thyroid carcinoma (parafollicular C cells) and MEN 2 (RET).
