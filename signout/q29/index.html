Key points
  • Bee sting in a sensitized patient: allergen cross-links IgE on FcεRI of mast cells (type I hypersensitivity).
  • Cross-linking activates phospholipase C: PIP₂ → IP3 (Ca²⁺ release from the ER) + DAG (protein kinase C). The Ca²⁺ rise triggers degranulation of preformed histamine and tryptase.
  • Trap: the COX pathway (B) makes prostaglandins, which are newly synthesized mediators, not preformed. Epinephrine works through β₂ → ↑ cAMP, which stabilizes mast cells (A is the treatment, not the trigger).
