Key points
  • Bloody diarrhea with tenesmus, crypt abscesses without granulomas, no fistulas or perianal disease and a “lead pipe” colon: ulcerative colitis (starts in the rectum, continuous, mucosa only).
  • Its classic extraintestinal association is primary sclerosing cholangitis (p-ANCA +), which raises the risk of cholangiocarcinoma. UC also raises colorectal cancer risk: surveillance colonoscopy.
  • Trap: B12 malabsorption (C), enterovesical fistula (B), oxalate stones (A) and terminal ileal strictures (E) are all Crohn disease, which is transmural and involves the terminal ileum. UC spares the ileum.
